# v1.5 Requirements — print-friendly-pdf

## R1 — Region-aware inversion masking ✅ IMPLEMENTED 2026-06-10 (deterministic mechanism; all acceptance criteria passed in-session; included in launch scope)

**Logged:** 2026-06-10
**Source:** v1 test on the validation document: a page with white logo cards on a dark background

**Defect in v1:** Global lightness inversion flips white content panels to
black. On that page, a column of white cards containing
brand logos — inverted to black cards with color-distorted logos. The
region became ink-heavier than the original and visually wrong.

**Requirement:** On pages selected for inversion, light content regions
(white/near-white panels, cards, callout boxes, embedded images on light
backgrounds) must be excluded from inversion. These regions keep their
original pixels — white background, original content colors.

**Acceptance criteria (test against the validation document's two panel pages):**
1. Page background and white text invert as in v1 (dark → light, white
   text → black)
2. White logo cards remain white with logos in original
   brand colors
3. White illustration panels remain white with original
   illustration colors
4. No visible seams or halos at region boundaries

**Candidate mechanisms (decide at v1.5 ADR time):**
- Deterministic: connected-component analysis on near-white pixels;
  mask components above an area threshold before applying inversion
- AI-assisted: browser-side Claude classification returns region bounding
  boxes per page (extends the existing v1.5 thumbnail-classification flow
  from page-level to region-level)

**Open question:** minimum region size threshold — small white elements
(bullets, icons) should still invert with the page; only substantial
panels should be preserved. Threshold to be tuned on real documents.

## R2 — Vector background removal path

**Logged:** 2026-06-11 · **Status:** Scoped, not started

**Finding:** Diagnostic on the validation document confirmed discrete
vector background rects (full-page filled rect, dark fill, identifiable
via `page.get_drawings()`). A vector-native path could whiten backgrounds
without rasterization, preserving searchable text at native resolution.

**Why it is not a quick win:** whitening the background alone reintroduces
the white-text-on-white failure. The vector path must also recolor text
fills — requiring content-stream parsing that distinguishes white-as-text
(invert to dark) from white-as-shape-fill (preserve), including inside
Form XObjects. Estimated 80+ lines, fragile across PDF generators.

**Acceptance criteria (when built):**
1. Background rects (>=80% page area, dark fill) become pure white
2. White text recolored dark; output text remains selectable/searchable
3. White cards/panels and embedded images unchanged
4. Falls back to the raster path when no vector background is found
5. Raster regression suite still passes

**Interim resolution (shipped 2026-06-11):** raster path now snaps light
near-neutral pixels to pure white post-inversion (L >= 0.85, chroma
<= 0.12), eliminating the pale-blue background tint. R2 remains the
path to searchable output.
